# Toddler AI Voice Teacher

A voice-first AI companion that teaches young children (roughly ages 2-5)
through spoken interaction — letters, numbers, colors, shapes, simple words,
and songs — rather than a screen-tap interface.

## Status

Early scaffolding. Requirements are still being defined.

## Requirements (to be filled in)

- Content: curriculum scope, lesson structure, progression/difficulty
- Safety: content moderation, data privacy for children (COPPA considerations),
